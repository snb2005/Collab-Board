Why this order?
Each phase builds directly on the previous one without introducing unnecessary dependencies:
Phase 1 establishes identity and security.
Phase 2 introduces collaboration boundaries (workspaces and permissions).
Phase 3 delivers a fully functional single-user whiteboard.
Phase 4 adds real-time capabilities using the existing whiteboard APIs.
Phase 5 layers advanced collaboration features on top of a stable collaborative core.
Phase 6 focuses entirely on engineering quality rather than new features.
By the end of every phase, you'll have a working, demoable product, making debugging easier and allowing you to showcase steady progress rather than waiting until the very end. This approach also keeps the architecture modular, so adding future features like AI assistance, templates, or plugins won't require major restructuring.
